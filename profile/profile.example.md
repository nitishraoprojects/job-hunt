# Candidate profile

Source of truth for every tailored resume, cover letter and message.
Tailoring may reword, reorder, emphasize and drop. It may only add things listed under "Facts".

## Facts
- Name · City · Phone · Email
- Current employer, title, dates
- Core skills (be honest about depth: "React (basic)" is fine)
- Measurable outcomes you can defend in an interview
- Notable projects
- Education
